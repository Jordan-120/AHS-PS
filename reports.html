Narratives

Narratives are required for arrests, use of force, hands-on applications, critical incidents, death or serious injury processes, property damage, MHA transfers, fines, and trespass orders.

When Unsure

If a scenario feels like it should be documented, discuss it with a TL or Manager and err on the side of caution.

Notebook Notes

Officer notebooks remain required and may be used as future reference for Protective Services incidents.
