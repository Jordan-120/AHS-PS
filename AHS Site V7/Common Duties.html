Essentials Guide

Pay & Payroll

Pay Stub Basics

A 12-hour shift is generally treated as 11 worked hours plus 1 paid lunch hour. You are still paid for lunch because you must remain available for calls, but shift diffs and premiums do not apply to that lunch hour.

Pay Increases

Pay steps are based on hours worked. Use ePeople to check your current step and how many hours remain before your next increase.

Schedule Periods

On the schedule, the shifts between the blue lines represent a pay period. The shifts between the black lines are working sets

Overtime

Overtime is based on averaging across set periods. You need over 28 shifts across 4 pay periods. The only exception is working more then 8 (so 9 or more) shifts in a single pay period. OT is paid at 2x base pay per hour (not including shift differentials/premiums)

IE: Working many shifts in a row does not mean overtime unless one of these threshold are met.

Shift Differential

Days: no premium
Evenings: 1500-2300 (+2.75)
Nights: 2300-0700 (+5.00)
Weekends: Friday at 1500 to Monday 0700 (+3.25)

Only applies when actually working. Vacation, sick time, personal leave, and banked time do not receive shift differential.

Stat Holidays

Stat days are paid at an increased rate, not including shift differential. Full-time meambers get 7.75 hours banked every STAT day even if not worked that STAT, both full-time and casual meambers are paid out if the STAT is worked.

STAT rate is 1.5x per hour, Super STAT (Christmas Day and the August Civic Holiday) is 2x per hour

Benefits & Leave (Full Time Only)

Vacation Time

Vacation is advanced so staff can use time before earning the full yearly amount. ePeople may show a negative balance early on. Vacation is earned gradually as you work.

Personal Leave

Personal leave can be used when something comes up and you need time away from work. It is counts only as 7 hours, but commonly topped up with vacation or banked stat time to cover a full shift.

Bereavement Leave

Bereavement leave may be available when an close family member passes away. Confirm eligibility and length with your manager.

Sick Time

Sick time is earned as you work and starts at zero. Check ePeople for your current sick bank balance.

Flex Account

Flex spending now $1,200, you decide how much goes into health spending and wellness spending. Health covers eligible medical costs (dental, massages, chiropractor etc) to cover the rest of what is short or max by blue cross. while wellness covers approved wellness-related items or services (Bikes, Gym Equipment, Computer stuff oddly enough). You choose how much you allocate into Health or Wellness, if not specified, it splits 50/50 to both accounts. It can also got into a set up TFSA or RRSP is designated.

it resets annually (Jan 1st), but does not roll over. Use it or lose it

Allowances

Boot allowance can used every two years. Meambers who have completed the bike course my also claim bike shoes. This is can be done every three years and covers $300, Submit receipts through the proper process.

Allowance is $250

Full-Time Issue

  • 4 uniform pants
  • 1 Peace Officer jacket
  • 1 forage cap
  • 1 tie

Maintain at least one long-sleeve shirt for court appearances.

Part-Time / Casual Issue

  • 2 uniform shirts
  • 2 uniform pants
  • 1 Peace Officer jacket
  • 1 forage cap
  • 1 tie

Standard Equipment

  • Inner/outer duty belt and keepers
  • Baton with holster
  • 2 handcuffs with cases and keys
  • Safety glasses, flashlight, rescue breather
  • Gloves, silent key holder, notebook, toque
1Identify damaged, soiled, defective, or missing item.
2Tell your Team Leader as soon as possible.
3Complete the Uniform/Equipment Order Form.
4Team Leader/Site Manager reviews and forwards to TDU.
5TDU fills the request or advises if backordered/unavailable.

Bike Patrol

Only active Bike Patrol members who have completed the AHS Bike Training Program are issued Bike Patrol uniform items.

Returns

When leaving employment, all issued uniform and equipment must be accounted for and returned. Return paperwork/checklists are used to verify all items.

Important

Do not keep more than the allotted number of uniform or equipment items. Excess items must be returned to the Training and Development Unit.
