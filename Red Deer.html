Regular individual

Contact the potors to see if there ready. If so meet them at the porters office and escourt them to the moruge. Common use of Authoritys

Form 10

This is a designated Mental Health Facility, You can Form 10 people if required. Do not Form 10 pataints, unless it has been disscused with staff that it is the best course of action. If you do, follow basic Procedures (fill out Form 10, Perpective file with attched Form 10, Notes etc.)

Tresspass

You can inform someone they are tresspased. They are then to be escourted off property. If they refuse, you can arrest them or contact RCMP It is preferd that they sign the Tresspass Form and are given a copy. This is not required and if they don't sign, just write RTS (refused to sign). If they return without DIRECTLY seeking medical care, you may arrest them or just kick them off property. If arrested, call RCMP to pick them up. Attch the Tresspass From to the perpective file.
