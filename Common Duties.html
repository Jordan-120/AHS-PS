Pay Stuff

Pay Stub Explained

When you work, you techncally only work 11 hours, 1 hour is "lunch". You get paid for that lunch as you always have to be availble for call, but you do not get bounus on that hour (shift diff, STAT etc)

Pay increass happen by time worked. Every 200? hours you get a dollar pay increass. You how many hours you have till you next pay bump on Epeople

Allowance, Equipment and Clothes

You can request new gear on xxxxxxx. Fill out the form and CC your manager. Your allocated 3 short sleeve shirts, 1 long sleeve shirt, 2 pants, 1 jacket and replacement on any gear damaged (gloves, handcuffs, baton holster etc).

Pay Periods

Looking at the Schedual, the shifts between the BLUE lines is a pay period

Overtime

It get complicated, but I'll dumb it down. You get OT when you work more then 24 shifts across 3 pay periods. This means you won't get OT if you worked 10 shifts straight, but only 2 shift last week.

Shift Diff

xxxx-xxxx = Day (Nadda), xxxx-xxxx = Evenings (+$3), xxxx-xxxx = Nights (+$5)

You only get shift diff if ACTUALLY working (banked time like vacca, sick, pl etc don't get shift diff)

STATS

Stat days are 2x pay (not including shift diff), banked if full time, paid out if casual

STAT days are marked on Schedual as pink day, but Augest (Heritage Day) and December (Xmas Day) are SUPER Stat (3.5x pay)

Full Time Benies

Vacca Time

You get 128 hours of Vacca time a year. If you look at your vacca time on epeople is probably say's -X hours. It starts like this so when you start, you have vacca hours immediately, rather then having to work like a year till you start banking it. You get essential get 1 hour of vacca added every shift. Max Vacca you can bank is +200, after that management is going to get on your ass to cash or use it.

Personal Leave

A day you can take whenever you want. It's meant as somthing came up and you need to skip work to deal with it. You can only use 7 hours of it at a time. This is often supplemented with 4 hours of banked STAT or Vacca time to equal a regular shift (again no shift diff).

Grival Leave

If a family meaber passes away you can take 2-3 days? Check with your Manager.

Sick Time

Banked with time worked. Unlike Vacca time, starts at 0. You get an hour every two shifts?

Flex Account

You get $900 in a Flex account each year. You must pick how much you want of the $900 you want in Wellness and Health. Health spending supplements medical stuff (dentist, messages, etc). Wellness is to buy stuff that essential would "help your Mental Health". There werid about what counts, you can't buy a TV, but you can buy a PC monitor. Essential just have tp justify how it will "Help" you "grow" or "stay fit".

Allowance

Every two years you can claim your boot allowance. When you buy new boots submit the recipet on XXXX. They will reimbure you up to $200. If you did the bike course you also get a shoe allowance, same thing but only covers $150.
