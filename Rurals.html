Every Shift Basics

Start of Shift

  • Gear up with keys, notebook, phone, and radio.
  • Join the Teams conference call at 06:40 or 18:45.
  • Sign on with PSCC using your name, call sign, and vehicle number.
  • Submit the pre-trip inspection on Teams.
  • Submit receipts on Teams, including fuel or car wash receipts.

Night Shift

Attend the Paladin conference call at 23:45. One Peace Officer leads with a safety topic for discussion.

Form Assessments

Assessment

Assess whether the individual needs a security watch. If they seem aggressive or likely to leave, have staff call for a security watch.

Complete a Perspective file, or ensure the guard completes one if applicable.

Documentation

A narrative is usually only needed if you go hands-on or something significant occurs. Message the TL with the file number.

Do not forget to search the individual and their belongings when appropriate. Many people carry weapons.

AGTA Forms

Treat Adult Guardianship and Trusteeship Act forms the same as a Form 1 for assessment and documentation purposes.
