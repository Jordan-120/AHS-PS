Form Assessments

Assess if they individual needs a Security Watch. If they seem agressive or likely to leave, have the staff call for a Security Watch. Make sure you (or the gaurd) do a Perspective file. Only do narrative unless you went hands on or something. Also don't forget to do a search of the individual/belongings (Many people carry weapons). Message the TL the file number. Treat AGTA (Adult Gaurdian Trustie Act) forms the same as a Form 1.
