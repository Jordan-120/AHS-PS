Safekeeping

Items

  • Log items in the safekeeping book.
  • Complete the required safekeeping entry.
  • Place items in the red cabinets accordingly.
  • Give the receipt page to the unit associated with the patient.

Money

  • Log money in the safekeeping book in the safe.
  • Complete the required safekeeping entry.
  • Place items in the red cabinets or safe accordingly.
  • Give the receipt page to the unit associated with the patient.

Important Notes

  • Do not edit sheets once completed.
  • No partial releases. Release everything and re-log if needed.
  • Editing sheets can be perceived as possible theft.
  • Do not hold illegal items, including illegal drugs or open liquor.
  • Do not release items unless the patient provides the receipt.

Morgue

Location

The morgue is on the lower level near the maintenance bay / housekeeping area.

Morgue Out

  • Check with Admissions for the funeral home's arrival time.
  • Wait at the loading bay near geriatrics for the funeral home vehicle.
  • Escort funeral home staff down to the morgue.
  • Escort them back to the vehicle with the body.
  • No paperwork is required unless otherwise directed.

Fire Alarm Pull Station

If an alarm is pulled, check whether it is real. If it is false, quickly reset the pull station when safe to do so.

If the station is not reset promptly, it can complicate the response and system status.
